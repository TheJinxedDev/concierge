# Concierge scoring-disabled policy

Numeric scoring and read-only score estimates are disabled in semantic beta. They are not required for setup, factual reads, proposal capture, MCP discovery, or fresh-agent verification.

A literal dated user rating remains a typed user event and preserves its provenance. A polarity balance, model confidence, tone, delayed eligibility rule, or assistant interpretation must never become a canonical numeric rating.

The delayed auto-promotion/scoring experiment remains preserved and quarantined. It is not package behavior and not semantic-beta evidence.

A future scoring phase requires a frozen evidence set plus measured accuracy, coverage, abstention, conflict handling, and correction behavior. Until that gate exists, the honest response is cited evidence or a clear statement that scoring is disabled.
